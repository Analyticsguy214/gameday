The case

Why Dallas, and why not

Working for SMU

  • No ACC campus yet. Nine stops in, GameDay has hosted at SEC, Big Ten and Big 12 schools only.
  • First-ever on-campus football show. SMU is one of the few Power Four schools that has never hosted.
  • Ranked vs. ranked, ACC title stakes. Both teams entered November at 6-1 chasing No. 1 Miami.
  • Homecoming crowd on Dallas Hall lawn, in a top-5 TV market.
  • Week 11 has repeat problems. Baton Rouge was the Week 1 stop and Bloomington is the Week 9 stop.

Working against

  • Texas at LSU is the blue-blood, playoff-eliminator game ESPN usually can't resist.
  • Utah at Arizona is a ranked pairing, though GameDay was in Utah two weeks running.
  • Neither team is top 10. Two ranked teams in the teens rarely win a crowded week.
  • One loss ends it. Any slip by SMU or Wake before Nov. 14 kills the case.
