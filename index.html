The case

Why Dallas, and why not

Working for SMU

  • No ACC campus yet. Nine stops in, GameDay has hosted at SEC, Big Ten and Big 12 schools only. The ACC is owed a trip.
  • First-ever on-campus football show. SMU is one of the few Power Four schools that has never hosted. GameDay likes a first-timer story (Cal 2024, Iowa City's return this year).
  • Ranked vs. ranked, ACC title stakes. Both teams are 6-1 and 4-1 in league play, chasing No. 1 Miami.
  • Homecoming crowd on Dallas Hall lawn, in a top-5 TV market.
  • Week 11 has repeat problems. Texas–LSU is in Baton Rouge (Week 1 stop) and USC–Indiana is in Bloomington (this week's stop).

Working against

  • Texas at LSU is the blue-blood, playoff-eliminator game ESPN usually can't resist.
  • Utah at Arizona is a top-25 pairing with an unbeaten Utah, though GameDay was in Utah two weeks running.
  • Neither team is top 10. Two ranked teams in the teens rarely win a crowded week.
  • One loss ends it. SMU has a Friday game Nov. 6, Wake hosts FCS Merrimack; any slip kills the case.
